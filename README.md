# Hollywood Animal Campaign Desk

Campaign Desk is an unofficial companion for planning film campaigns in Hollywood Animal. It ranks advertisers, shows which audiences and holidays fit your film, and estimates the total screenings and rentals for each week.

[Open Campaign Desk](https://santanudatta.github.io/HA-Campaign-Desk/)

## Plan a campaign

1. Add your film's tags under **Story elements**, or select **Try an example** to see a sample campaign.
2. Enter the film's **Commercial** and **Artistic** scores. If you select multiple genres, they split 100% evenly. You can adjust the percentages to match your film.
3. Check **Advertiser picks** for ranked agencies. Open **Campaign breakdown** for audience matches, holiday bonuses, and suggested campaign timing.
4. Enter the **Owned screenings** you can assign to the film each week. The **Distribution calculator** shows the weekly target and how many extra screenings to rent.

Your film is saved in your browser. Select **Clear film** to start over.
