        How the estimate works

        Week 1 is 2,000 screenings per Commercial score point. Week 2 is 1,000 per point. From Week 3 through Week 8, each week’s total is 20% lower than the previous week’s.

        Owned screenings cover as much of each week’s total as possible. Rentals cover the remaining screenings. Both bars use the largest weekly total as their scale.
